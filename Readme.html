To Maryam  ✦

When you said goodbye, I felt it. Like something inside me just went quiet. That moment was not small to me — it was heavy, and it stayed heavy.

I tried to stop you. I meant every single word I said. But at some point, I got scared. I didn't want to keep pushing if it was making things worse. So I went quiet. And I know now — to you, that looked like I gave up.

I didn't give up, Maryam. I just didn't know how to stay without feeling like I was being too much. That was my mistake, and I own it fully.

The next morning, when you told me it was a test — I didn't feel angry. I felt sad. Not because you tested me, but because I failed to show you what you already deserved to see: that I would not let you go easily.

You are not just someone I talk to every day. You are someone who makes my day feel different — better — just by being in it. I didn't say that enough. I should have.

You were right to feel disappointed. You wanted proof that you matter to me, and I got quiet at the exact moment I should have been loud. That won't happen again.

I'm sorry, Maryam. Not the kind of sorry that just wants things to go back to normal — the kind that actually understands what went wrong. The kind that wants to do better.

You are worth fighting for. You are worth showing up for. And I'm showing up now — even if it's a little late.

Always yours, Hasnat
Made with the things I didn't say loudly enough.  ✦
